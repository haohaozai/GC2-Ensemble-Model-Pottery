Scores. Every 🏺 similar pottery search is checked automatically and added up here: did a matching pot show up near the top? Glaze recipe searches are logged separately and are not in this table.

?How to read the scores

✓ match / ✗ no matchA result matches on shape when it has the same form (cup = cup), on glaze when its surface group is the same (glossy / satin / matte), on colour when it is within ΔE 20 of your colour. Strict = all three at once.
Hit Rate@KIn how many of your searches was at least one matching pot in the top K? 90 % at K = 3: nine searches in ten had a match in the first three.
P@K (precision)Of the top K pots, how many match? 7 of the top 10 = 70 %.
nDCG0 to 1: are the best matches at the very top? 1 = the best possible order.
Recall@KOnly when you typed the true Glazy recipe ID: how many of that recipe's own pots reached the top K.

Session evaluation all searches in work/queries/session_log.json

Hit Rate@K = share of searches with at least one relevant result in the top K (this was called "Recall@K" before). Precision@K = share of the top K that is relevant. Recall@K = share of all relevant objects that reach the top K — shown only for the true recipe, whose objects are the complete relevant set.

How to use. Five steps, then real examples: what the app found for each photo, the top recipes and pots, and what every number means.

🚀Quick start

  1. Start the app: double-click RUN_GLAZE_SEARCH.bat and keep its black window open. This page opens by itself (http://127.0.0.1:8770).
  2. 📷 My photo: drop, paste or browse a photo, or click one of the examples below. Click the pot you mean (yellow outline).
  3. Read what the app found: the glaze colour, the shape and the surface, each with how sure the model is.
  4. 🧪 Glaze recipes: press Find glaze recipes. Add words (satin, cone 6) to steer it. Open a recipe with View on Glazy.
  5. 🏺 Similar pottery: press Find similar pottery, try the presets (Balanced, Shape, Glaze, Colour), and see ✓ / ✗ for each pot.
