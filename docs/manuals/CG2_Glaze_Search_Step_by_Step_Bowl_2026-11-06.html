Part D · Two things to know

9 · The formula from the pasted example is not used

Sent with the photo and the sentence, smart search answers: formula ignored: 3 oxide(s), no flux - a formula needs at least 3 oxides including a flux (Na2O, K2O, Li2O, CaO, MgO, ZnO, BaO, SrO …). The results are the same as 8b. A formula needs at least 3 oxides including a flux (CaO, K₂O, Na₂O, MgO, ZnO …). Colour match never takes a formula: its chemistry is only on the recipe side.

10 · A colour word in the text replaces the photo's colour, so leave it out when you use a photo

On the page, "Read it" copies the text's colour into the colour box: "deep blu" → #0000FF replaces the measured #0E1F3E. The same search as 8b then gives:
